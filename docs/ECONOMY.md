# Economy

Currencies: **Gold** (spend) and **Forge Essence** (legendary evolution). Items are never currencies.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Shop offers | 6 (+ShopOffers stat, 3..8); rarity weights by chapter; compatibility-weighted; 1 "build memory" slot (30%) biased to the closest/favourite recipe |
| Pity | <2 weapons → a Frame; coreless weapon → a Core; no board piece after the first battle → a piece |
| Price | base cost × rarity (1.0/1.15/1.4/1.8) × (1 − discount) |
| Sell | 50% of base cost; whole weapons sell for the sum of their parts |
| Reroll | 8 + 4 per reroll this visit, capped at 40; locks persist through rerolls and into the next shop |
| Temper | Forge node: first action free; tier 2 = 55 g, tier 3 = 110 g; shop temper +15 g |
| Board expand | Forge action, 70 g after the free action; 5×4 → 7×5 |
| Rewards | battle gold = enemy gold + 6/15/35 (battle/elite/boss); essence 1 (elite) / 2 (boss); skip reward = +12 g |
| Field repair | +15% max HP after a won battle; boss +30% |
